οἰκονομία — the care of a household

Money is something entrusted to us. Oikonomia helps your household look after it well — in ordinary language, on an ordinary phone.

Continue with Google

Free. No subscription, no adverts, and no bank login ever asked for.

Your statements never leave your phone

Oikonomia reads your bank statements on your own device — PDF or CSV, from any Indian bank, password-protected or not. The file itself is never sent anywhere, and neither is the password.

Figures you can check

Every statement is re-added and checked against the closing balance your bank printed. If anything disagrees, Oikonomia says so instead of handing you numbers that merely look right.

A plan built from what you actually spend

Not a rule about what families ought to spend. Rent, groceries, medicine and school fees are protected first; whatever is left is given somewhere useful to go.

Never a lecture

Oikonomia will not shame you for debt or for a hard month. Where money is short, it says so plainly — that is a gap in income, not a failure of character.

Grounded in stewardship

Biblical principles about provision, planning, debt, contentment and generosity shape how Oikonomia advises — kept clearly apart from modern financial practice, and never presented as commands about amounts.

How it works

  1. Sign in and create your household, or join one with its code.
  2. Add what you spend as you go — by typing, or just by saying it.
  3. Upload your bank statements whenever it suits you.
  4. Oikonomia shows what it understood, and waits for you to agree.
  5. Together you make a plan, and it keeps you company through the month.

Oikonomia gives household guidance, not professional financial, tax or investment advice. Figures it reads can be wrong, which is why it shows you where each one came from.
